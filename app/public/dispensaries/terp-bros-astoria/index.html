Terp Bros - Astoria

Terp Bros is on Ditmars Boulevard in Astoria, near the end of the N and W line, and it is the largest licensed dispensary we track in Queens' most dispensary-dense neighborhood, with about 800 products from 116 brands. The menu is evenly split: roughly 210 vapes, 206 pre-rolls, 187 flower options and 151 edibles, with a small concentrate shelf and a handful of tinctures and topicals.

That balance makes it the easiest Astoria shop to use for a mixed basket. Ayrloom drinks and gummies, MFNY live resin, STIIIZY vapes, Jetty Extracts cartridges, Dank By Definition flower and Jaunty all-in-ones each have 23 to 37 listings. It also has one of the lowest entry prices in the borough: Ayrloom Honeycrisp cider at $3.54 and TUNE seltzers at $5.31. Cannavita on Steinway Street, a few minutes away, is the other big Astoria option with about 730 products.

Terp Bros - Astoria FAQ
Terp Bros or Cannavita — which Astoria dispensary has more?

They are close. Terp Bros lists about 800 products and 116 brands; Cannavita on Steinway Street lists around 730 and 105. Terp Bros is slightly stronger in vapes, Cannavita in pre-rolls and MFNY products. They are a short trip apart, so comparing both is easy.

Does Terp Bros carry live resin?

Yes. MFNY, which specializes in live resin vapes, tinctures and concentrates, has about 34 listings here, and Jetty Extracts adds a cartridge line. The concentrate shelf itself is small at around 28 items.

What is the nearest subway?

The shop is at 36-10 Ditmars Boulevard, a few blocks from the Astoria-Ditmars Boulevard station at the end of the N and W lines.
