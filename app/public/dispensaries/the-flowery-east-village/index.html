The Flowery - East Village

The Flowery's East Village store is at 101 East 10th Street, near Third Avenue, with about 505 products from 64 brands. Its menu is evenly balanced: edibles (138), pre-rolls (129), vapes (124) and flower (102) are all within a few dozen listings of one another, which makes it a reliable one-stop shop in a neighborhood where several competitors specialize.

Ayrloom and To The Moon lead with 28 listings each, giving it strong drinks and New York-grown flower, followed by MFNY, Flav, STIIIZY and Off Hours. The entry price is $4.42 for LEVEL Slumber tablets, with Cycling Frog seltzers and Ayrloom drinks at $5.31. The Flowery also has stores in the West Village, SoHo, the Upper West Side and Williamsburg; this is the second-largest after the Upper West Side location.

The Flowery - East Village FAQ
Is this a good all-around East Village dispensary?

Yes. Its four main categories are all between 100 and 140 listings, so it covers edibles, pre-rolls, vapes and flower without a weak spot. Gotham Bowery is bigger on edibles and PRYZM on pre-rolls, but The Flowery is the most balanced of the three.

What is To The Moon?

To The Moon is a New York flower brand. The Flowery East Village carries 28 of its products, one of the deeper selections in Manhattan, alongside Ayrloom drinks and gummies at the same depth.

What is the cheapest item?

LEVEL 5 Slumber tablet two-packs at $4.42 before tax, then Cycling Frog Black Currant 1:1 seltzers and Ayrloom Black Cherry drinks at $5.31.
