Culture House - Greenpoint

807 Manhattan Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11222Directions in Google Maps →

Culture House Greenpoint is at 807 Manhattan Avenue, on Greenpoint's main shopping street in north Brooklyn. It is one of three licensed dispensaries we track in Greenpoint, with about 390 products from 70 brands at last count. Milligrams, a short walk away on Greenpoint Avenue, and Green Apple Distributors farther south on Manhattan Avenue both list somewhat more, but the three are close enough in size that the real difference between them is what each one stocks rather than how much.

Culture House leans hard toward edibles: 143 listings, the most of any Greenpoint shop we track, followed by 106 vapes and 83 pre-rolls. Flower is the thinnest section in the neighborhood at 36 options, so flower shoppers may do better at Green Apple. Jaunty is the biggest brand with 31 products, then Fernway with 23, followed by Ayrloom, PAX, Florist Farms and Camino. The entry price is $5, shared by 1906 Chill and Go tablet two-packs, Jaunty Sour Watermelon 2:1 CBD:THC gummies and a LayUp Black Cherry Lemonade drink.

Culture House - Greenpoint FAQ
Where is Culture House Greenpoint?

At 807 Manhattan Avenue in Greenpoint, Brooklyn, a short walk from Milligrams at 58 Greenpoint Avenue. Green Apple Distributors, the third Greenpoint shop we track, is farther south at 572 Manhattan Avenue.

What does Culture House Greenpoint carry most?

Edibles, with 143 listings, then vapes (106) and pre-rolls (83). Jaunty and Fernway have the most products of any brand on the menu.

What is the cheapest item at Culture House Greenpoint?

At last check the menu started at $5, a price shared by 1906 tablet two-packs, Jaunty gummy two-packs and a LayUp drink. Prices on Sensei are menu prices before New York's cannabis tax.
