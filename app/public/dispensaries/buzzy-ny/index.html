Buzzy NY

Buzzy NY is at 137 Court Street in Brooklyn Heights, near Atlantic Avenue and a short walk from Borough Hall. It stocks about 380 products from 79 brands and is the second-largest of the four licensed dispensaries we track in Brooklyn Heights and Downtown Brooklyn. Edibles lead at 111 listings, with 81 pre-rolls, 77 vapes and 72 flower options. Its 29-item concentrate case is the largest in the immediate area.

Jaunty is the deepest brand at 23 products, followed by MFNY, Heavy Hitters, Fernway, Rove and Camino. That mix leans toward vapes and gummies, with MFNY supplying most of the concentrates. The entry price is $5 for a Tune blueberry lemonade can or Ayrloom's root beer, half-and-half and lemonade drinks. The Travel Agency's Downtown Brooklyn store on Fulton is the larger menu nearby; Buzzy is the closer option for anyone coming from Cobble Hill or the Court Street side of the Heights.

Buzzy NY FAQ
What does Buzzy NY carry that nearby shops do not?

Concentrates. With 29 listings, mostly from MFNY, it has the deepest dab and live resin selection among the Brooklyn Heights and Downtown dispensaries we track. It also carries Fernway and Rove vapes.

Is Buzzy close to the subway?

Yes. Court Street is served by the 2, 3, 4, 5 and R trains at Borough Hall and Court Street, a few minutes' walk north of the shop.

How much cannabis can I buy at once?

New York's possession limit for adults is three ounces of flower and 24 grams of concentrate. Dispensaries sell within those limits per transaction, and product is lab-tested and labeled with THC content.
