Sweetlife

Sweetlife is at 1662 First Avenue on the Upper East Side, near 86th Street, and with about 930 products from 114 brands it is the larger of the two licensed dispensaries we track in the neighborhood. Pre-rolls are its biggest category at around 280 listings, with vapes close behind at 247, then 180 edibles and 169 flower options, plus a 36-item concentrate shelf.

The brand depth is unusual for the Upper East Side. EUREKA, a vape maker, has 77 listings here, the most of any brand at the shop, and Rove adds another 60, so between them Sweetlife has one of the strongest vape walls east of Central Park. Jeeter and STIIIZY cover pre-rolls and all-in-ones, Ruby Farms supplies New York-grown pre-rolls, and Smokiez handles gummies. The cheapest items are Ayrloom drinks at $5.

Sweetlife FAQ
What is Sweetlife known for?

Depth in vapes. EUREKA and Rove together account for well over 130 listings, which is unusual for a single shop, and Jeeter and STIIIZY add more. It is the Upper East Side shop to visit if you want to compare vape brands side by side.

How does it compare to The Emerald Dispensary?

Sweetlife carries more than twice as many products, about 930 against roughly 420 at The Emerald on Lexington Avenue. The Emerald does list more brands relative to its size and is worth a look for edibles; Sweetlife wins on vapes and pre-rolls.

What ID do I need to buy here?

A valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. A driver's license, state ID or passport all work. Licensed New York dispensaries check ID at the door, and there is no medical card requirement for adult-use shops.
