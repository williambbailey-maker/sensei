Purple Buds, Inc - Recreational

Purple Buds is at 75-20 Roosevelt Avenue in Jackson Heights, under the elevated 7 train, with about 525 products from 78 brands. It is the larger of the two licensed dispensaries we track in the neighborhood and a pre-roll shop first: about 210 of its listings are pre-rolls, with 110 vapes, 99 edibles and 86 flower options behind them.

Hashtag Honey (39) and Ruby Farms (38), both New York brands built around pre-rolls, are the deepest lines here, with Ayrloom drinks, Claybourne Co., Hepworth flower and Revert rounding out the list. The entry price is among the lowest in Queens at $3.10 for a 1906 Boost pouch, with Ayrloom drinks at $4.42. ENFLOR, a few blocks north on 85th Street, is the other Jackson Heights option with a similar size and an even more New York-focused brand list.

Purple Buds, Inc - Recreational FAQ
Purple Buds or ENFLOR — what is the difference?

They are similar in size (about 525 and 500 products). Purple Buds has more pre-rolls and flower and a lower entry price; ENFLOR carries more vapes and tinctures and goes deeper on New York brands like Florist Farms. They are a few blocks apart.

What is the cheapest thing on the menu?

At last check, a 1906 Boost two-pack at $3.10 before tax, followed by Ayrloom Cranberry Apple and Black Cherry drinks at $4.42.

Is it easy to reach by subway?

Yes. The shop is on Roosevelt Avenue near the 74th Street-Broadway and Jackson Heights-Roosevelt Avenue stations, served by the 7, E, F, M and R lines.
