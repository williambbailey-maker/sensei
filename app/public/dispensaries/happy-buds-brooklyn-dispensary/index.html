Happy Buds Brooklyn - Dispensary

Happy Buds Brooklyn is at 225 Malcolm X Boulevard in Bed-Stuy, near Hancock Street and a short walk from the Utica Avenue A and C station. It is the only licensed dispensary we track in Bed-Stuy, with about 370 products from 47 brands. Pre-rolls are the clear focus at 153 listings, followed by 89 flower options, 84 edibles and 38 vapes. Concentrates and tinctures are minimal.

The brand list is unusual for the city. Dank By Definition leads at 35 products and Revert Cannabis has 32, both New York growers that specialize in pre-rolls and flower, while MFNY, Ayrloom, Ruby Farms and mini mart fill in the rest. The entry price is $5 for Weed Water's sparkling sativa cans in razz mint limeade, pink lemonade, yuzu peach and P.O.G. Wowie. For a bigger vape selection, Stash Master in Bushwick lists about three times as many; for pre-rolls and flower in central Brooklyn, Happy Buds is the stronger stop.

Happy Buds Brooklyn - Dispensary FAQ
What is Happy Buds best known for?

Pre-rolls and New York flower. With 153 pre-roll listings and Dank By Definition and Revert Cannabis as its deepest brands, it carries more joints than any other category and more state-grown flower than most shops its size.

Does Happy Buds have a good vape selection?

It is modest at 38 listings. For cartridges and all-in-ones in central Brooklyn, Stash Master in Bushwick and RNR Dispensary both list far more.

Is Happy Buds licensed by the state?

Yes. It holds a license from New York's Office of Cannabis Management, which means lab-tested products, ID checks at the door and the standard 13 percent tax at the register.
