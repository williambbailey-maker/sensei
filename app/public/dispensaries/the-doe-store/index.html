The Travel Agency Union Square

The Travel Agency's Union Square store is at 835 Broadway near 13th Street, with about 580 products from 61 brands, the largest of three licensed dispensaries we track around Gramercy and Union Square. The menu is evenly built, with vapes (152), pre-rolls (146) and edibles (137) within a few listings of each other, about 100 flower options and a 26-item concentrate shelf.

RYTHM is the deepest brand here at 55 listings, with Select, Heavy Hitters, Ruby Farms, Grassroots and MFNY behind it, the same established-brand mix found at the group's SoHo and Downtown Brooklyn stores. The cheapest items are Harney Brothers coffee shots, Ayrloom drinks and TUNE seltzers at $5. Green Genius on 3rd Avenue, a few blocks east, carries more brands and a deeper vape wall; Smiley Exotics on East 30th Street is the smaller third option.

The Travel Agency Union Square FAQ
How does this compare to Green Genius nearby?

The Travel Agency Union Square has slightly more products (about 580 to 549) and a more balanced shelf. Green Genius lists more brands (107 to 61) and leans toward vapes and edibles with very few pre-rolls. Pick The Travel Agency for pre-rolls, Green Genius for vape variety.

What brands does it carry the most of?

RYTHM with 55 listings, then Select (45), Heavy Hitters (39), Ruby Farms (36), Grassroots (26) and MFNY (19). It is a good stop for comparing several national flower and vape brands in one place.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

The store's menu is published online, which is where Sensei's prices come from, and The Travel Agency's stores take online orders for in-store pickup. Confirm current options on the shop's own menu page.
