Sheepshead Bay · Brooklyn

Twenty8gramz

Twenty8gramz is on Avenue U in Sheepshead Bay, a stretch of southern Brooklyn that has quietly become one of the city's densest dispensary clusters. It is the biggest of the three licensed shops we track in the neighborhood, with about 1,230 products from 140 brands, and its shelf leans hard toward vapes: roughly 440 vape listings, by far its largest category, followed by 280 pre-rolls, 215 flower options and just over 200 edibles.

If you vape, this is the Sheepshead Bay shop to start with. STIIIZY alone has nearly 70 listings here, and Claybourne Co., Ayrloom, Off Hours, Cannabals and Boutiq round out a vape and edible selection that is unusually deep for a shop outside Manhattan. Flower shoppers get a solid 215 options but should also look at Quality Control NY a few blocks away on Coney Island Avenue. The cheapest items on the menu are infused drinks from High Peaks and Ayrloom at $4.50 to $5.

Twenty8gramz FAQ
Which Sheepshead Bay dispensary has the most selection?

Twenty8gramz, with about 1,230 in-stock products. Quality Control NY on Coney Island Avenue is next at roughly 980, and Greene Street on Nostrand Avenue carries around 340. Together the three give Sheepshead Bay one of the largest combined menus in Brooklyn.

Is Twenty8gramz good for flower, or mostly vapes?

It is strongest in vapes, with around 440 listings. Flower is still well covered at about 215 options, but if flower is the priority it is worth comparing against Quality Control NY, which is a short walk away and keeps a similar count.

What does the 13 percent tax mean for the prices I see?

New York adds a 9 percent state excise tax and a 4 percent local tax to adult-use cannabis at checkout. Menu prices on Sensei are pre-tax, so a $5 drink rings up around $5.65 and a $50 eighth around $56.50.
