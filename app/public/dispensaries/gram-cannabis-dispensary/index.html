Grams Cannabis - Dispensary

360 Graham Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11211Directions in Google Maps →

Grams Cannabis is at 360 Graham Avenue in Williamsburg, near Conselyea Street and a block from the Graham Avenue L station. It carries about 420 products from 54 brands, one of the larger of the six licensed dispensaries we track in the neighborhood. The menu is evenly split: 106 pre-rolls, 103 flower options, 98 edibles and 77 vapes, plus a concentrate case of 27 items that is one of the largest in Williamsburg.

The defining feature is Florist Farms, stocked at 66 products, the deepest run of that New York brand at any licensed dispensary we track. Jetty Extracts, BLOOM, Jeeter and the shop's own Grams house label fill out the top of the brand list. The entry price is $5.31 for Layup raspberry and mango tea cans, Tune's Sicilian lemon and rose seltzer and Muz Muz two-pack gummies. If Florist Farms pre-rolls or vapes are what you buy, there is no better single shelf in the city.

Grams Cannabis - Dispensary FAQ
Why does Grams carry so much Florist Farms?

With 66 Florist Farms listings, it stocks a big slice of its whole menu from that one New York brand, covering pre-rolls, vapes, edibles and flower. No other licensed dispensary we track comes close, so it is the place to compare the full line.

What is Grams, the brand?

Grams lists its own house-label products alongside the other brands, about 40 listings under the Grams name. House labels are usually priced below equivalent branded items.

Does Grams have concentrates?

Yes, 27 listings, which is one of the largest dab and live resin cases among the Williamsburg shops we track. Jetty Extracts is one of the main concentrate brands stocked.
