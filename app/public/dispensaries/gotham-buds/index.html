Gotham Buds

Gotham Buds is at 248 West 125th Street in Harlem, steps from the Apollo Theater, with about 505 products from 64 brands. It is one of two licensed dispensaries we track in Harlem and Washington Heights, and its menu is pre-roll first: 181 pre-roll listings, with edibles (128), flower (96) and vapes (96) behind them. It lists no concentrates and only a handful of tinctures and topicals.

Jaunty is the deepest brand at 43 listings, mostly all-in-one vapes and gummies, with Rove vapes, Hashtag Honey and Dank By Definition for pre-rolls and flower, and Ayrloom and Grön for drinks and edibles. The entry price is $4.99 for a Cannabals LayUp Black Cherry Lemonade drink. Broadway Strains at 3858 Broadway in Washington Heights is the other uptown option, with a flower-first menu of about 500 products.

Gotham Buds FAQ
Gotham Buds or Broadway Strains?

Gotham Buds for pre-rolls (181 to 100) and convenience on 125th Street; Broadway Strains for flower (146 to 96) and a wider brand list. They are about 35 blocks apart, both near the 1 and A trains.

Does Gotham Buds carry concentrates?

Not at last check. The menu is pre-rolls, edibles, flower and vapes. For concentrates uptown, Broadway Strains lists about 15.

How do I get there?

The shop is on 125th Street between Adam Clayton Powell Jr. and Frederick Douglass Boulevards, a block or two from the 125th Street stations on the A, B, C, D and 2, 3 lines.
