ayrloom in NYC

Ayrloom is the most widely stocked cannabis brand in New York City. It is carried at 63 of the 67 licensed dispensaries we track, with about 1,400 products on shelves, more than any other label. The brand comes from the family behind the Beak & Skiff apple orchard in central New York, which explains the line: cider-style drinks, gummies and a long list of 2:1 and 1:1 THC-to-CBD beverages in flavors like Honeycrisp, cranberry apple, lemonade, root beer and vanilla cola.

Edibles are the core at 618 listings, mostly drinks and gummies, followed by 403 vapes, 150 pre-rolls, 123 tinctures and 101 topicals. That tincture and topical depth is why Ayrloom tops both of those categories citywide. Prices start at $3.39, and the single-serve 10-milligram drinks run $5 to $6 at most shops, which makes them the most common entry-level item on New York menus. The deepest Ayrloom runs are at NY Canna Co in Midtown with 49 products, Quality Control in Sheepshead Bay with 47 and The Flowery Upper West Side with 43.

ayrloom FAQ
What does 2:1 mean on Ayrloom drinks?

Two parts THC to one part CBD, usually 10 milligrams THC and 5 milligrams CBD per can. The CBD softens the effect, which is why these are a common first purchase. Ayrloom also makes 1:1 versions at 5 milligrams THC.

Is Ayrloom a New York brand?

Yes. It is grown and made in New York by the family behind the Beak & Skiff orchard near Syracuse. Licensed New York dispensaries can only sell cannabis grown and processed in the state, so every Ayrloom product on a New York shelf is made here.

Where can I find the most Ayrloom products?

NY Canna Co on 5th Avenue in Midtown lists 49, Quality Control in Sheepshead Bay 47 and The Flowery Upper West Side 43. Almost every licensed shop carries at least the drinks.
