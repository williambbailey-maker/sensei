Jaunty in NYC

Jaunty is a New York vape brand and the most stocked vaporizer label in the city, with 626 vape listings, ahead of STIIIZY and Fernway. It is carried at 41 of the 67 licensed dispensaries we track with about 790 products overall, including 118 edibles, 32 concentrates and a few pre-rolls. The signature format is the all-in-one, often in 1.5-gram sizes, with strain names like Blue Dream and Alien OG, plus gummies that have become a common low-price item.

Prices start at $4.52 for two-pack gummies, which is why Jaunty appears in the entry-price slot at so many shops, while the 1.5-gram all-in-ones run about $55 before tax. The deepest runs are at The Flowery Upper West Side and Highest Standards with 45 products each, Gotham Buds in Harlem with 43, Qube in Midtown with 40, ENFLOR in Jackson Heights with 37 and Stash Master in Bushwick with 35. The Flowery UWS, Highest Standards, ENFLOR and Stash Master all start Jaunty at $5 or less.

Jaunty FAQ
What is a Jaunty all-in-one?

A disposable vape with the battery and oil in one unit, no cartridge to swap. Jaunty sells them in 1.5-gram sizes at about $55, larger than the typical one-gram unit, in a wide range of strains.

Is Jaunty a New York company?

Yes. Jaunty is a New York brand, and everything on a licensed New York shelf is grown and made in the state. Its gummies, including the Dreamberry sleep formula with CBN, are among the cheapest edibles in the city.

Which shop has the most Jaunty?

The Flowery Upper West Side and Highest Standards in FiDi tie at 45 products each. Gotham Buds, Qube, ENFLOR and Stash Master each carry 35 or more.
