Florist Farms in NYC

Florist Farms is a New York grower from Cortland, carried at 79 of the 126 licensed dispensaries we track with about 1,250 products. It is one of the broadest home-grown lines: 509 pre-rolls, 378 vapes, 236 edibles and 130 flower listings, with the pre-rolls including live resin infused singles and five-packs. It is one of the most stocked pre-roll brands in the city.

Prices start at $5.31, with infused pre-roll singles, five-packs and eighths of flower each a step up before tax. The brand's depth is concentrated at a few shops: Grams in Williamsburg lists 66 products, the most anywhere, ENFLOR in Jackson Heights 35 and The Flowery Upper West Side 43, and Stoops in Chelsea, Urban Leaf in Midtown and The Flowery West Village also carry it. ENFLOR, Stoops and Urban Leaf list low entry prices for the brand, with ENFLOR from $5.90.

Florist Farms FAQ
Where is Florist Farms from?

Cortland, in central New York. It grows its own flower and uses it across its pre-rolls, vapes and edibles, which is why the line spans so many categories.

What is a live resin infused pre-roll?

A joint with live resin extract added to the flower, raising potency and aroma. Florist Farms sells them as singles and in five-packs of half-gram joints.

Which shop has the most Florist Farms?

Right now it's OC Dispensary, with 73 Florist Farms products. The deepest runs overall are at OC Dispensary (73), Grams Cannabis (66) and Dankley (48).
