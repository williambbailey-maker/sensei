Cannabals in NYC

Cannabals is a brand built around liquid diamonds, a high-potency extract, used in both its edibles and its vapes. It is carried at 39 of the 67 licensed dispensaries we track with about 400 products: 241 edibles, 136 vapes and 24 pre-rolls. The vapes are mostly two-gram disposables in strain names like Acapulco Gold and flavor names like Apple Burst and Baja Blast, and the edibles are gummies at a range of doses.

Prices start at $4.99 for single gummies, while the two-gram liquid diamond disposables run $73 to $80 before tax, so the brand covers both ends of the price range. The deepest runs are at NY Canna Co in Midtown with 37 products, Superfly on the Upper West Side with 33, Twenty8gramz in Sheepshead Bay with 31, Quality Control with 26, Stoops in Chelsea with 22 and Gotham Buds in Harlem with 22. Gotham Buds lists the lowest Cannabals entry price at $4.99.

Cannabals FAQ
What are liquid diamonds?

A vape oil made by re-liquefying THCA crystals, often called diamonds, with the strain's own terpenes. The result is very high potency with strong flavor. Cannabals uses it in its disposables and in the extract for its gummies.

Why are Cannabals vapes two grams?

The brand's standard disposable is a two-gram unit, double the usual one-gram size, which is why the $73 to $80 price looks high next to one-gram competitors. Per gram it is closer to the market.

Where can I find Cannabals gummies cheaply?

Gotham Buds in Harlem lists the brand from $4.99 and Superfly and Quality Control from $17 for full packs. NY Canna Co in Midtown has the widest selection at 37 products.
