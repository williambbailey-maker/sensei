Weed in Sheepshead Bay

Sheepshead Bay has three licensed dispensaries with about 2,550 products, the deepest neighborhood in Brooklyn and third-deepest in the city after Midtown and Chelsea. Twenty8gramz on Avenue U is the largest at about 1,230 products, the biggest licensed menu in Brooklyn. Quality Control on Coney Island Avenue lists about 980, and Greene Street on Nostrand Avenue near Avenue U about 340. All three are within a 15-minute walk along the Avenue U corridor.

Vapes lead at 814 listings, the second-largest vape total of any neighborhood, with 654 pre-rolls, 461 edibles and 460 flower options. The 129-item concentrate selection is the second-largest in the city after Midtown. Twenty8gramz carries the deepest Off Hours run we track at 38 products plus strong Ayrloom, Cannabals and Fernway selections; Quality Control has the deepest Grön run in the city and a strong MFNY and Ayrloom lineup. Prices start at $5 for Ayrloom drinks at Twenty8gramz and $6 at Greene Street. For southern Brooklyn, this is the destination neighborhood.

Sheepshead Bay FAQ
What is the biggest dispensary in Brooklyn?

Twenty8gramz at 2201 Avenue U in Sheepshead Bay, with about 1,230 products in our data. Stash Master in Bushwick is second at about 1,170.

Is Sheepshead Bay good for concentrates?

Yes. With 129 listings across three shops it trails only Midtown for dabs and live resin. Twenty8gramz and Quality Control carry most of them, with MFNY among the main brands.

Can I drive to these shops?

All three are on major avenues with street parking, and Quality Control is on Coney Island Avenue near the Belt Parkway. Sensei does not track lots, so check each store's listing for parking details.
