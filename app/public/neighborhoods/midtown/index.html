Weed in Midtown

Midtown has seven licensed dispensaries and about 4,740 products, the deepest neighborhood menu in New York by a wide margin. Qube on Broadway at 39th Street is the largest licensed dispensary in the city at more than 2,150 products from 249 brands. NY Canna Co on 5th Avenue at 36th lists about 780, The Daily Green on 7th Avenue in Times Square about 530, and Culture House near Herald Square, Torches across from the main library, Urban Leaf in Midtown East and Nicklz in the Theater District range from about 190 to 380.

Vapes lead at 1,410 listings, followed by 1,216 pre-rolls, 1,121 edibles and 723 flower options, with 184 concentrates, 57 tinctures and 32 topicals, each the largest total of any neighborhood. Qube alone carries 650 pre-rolls and 650 vapes. NY Canna Co has the deepest Ayrloom and Cannabals runs in the city, and The Daily Green is a strong MFNY and Off Hours stop. Prices start at $3 for 1906 Boost pills at Qube. Because every shop is within a 15-minute walk of Times Square, Penn Station or Grand Central, Midtown is the easiest place in the city to compare several menus in one trip.

Midtown FAQ
What is the largest dispensary in New York City?

Qube at 1412 Broadway near 39th Street, with more than 2,150 products from 249 brands in our data. It lists roughly twice as many products as the next-largest shop in the city.

Which Midtown dispensary is closest to Times Square?

The Daily Green at 719 7th Avenue is in Times Square itself, with about 530 products. Nicklz on 8th Avenue at 48th and Qube on Broadway at 39th are both within a five-minute walk.

Is there a dispensary near Grand Central?

Torches at 12 East 42nd Street, across from the New York Public Library, is about five minutes west of Grand Central with about 370 products and a strong vape selection. Urban Leaf on 2nd Avenue at 52nd serves Midtown East.
