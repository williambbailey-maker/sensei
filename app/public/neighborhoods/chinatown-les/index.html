Weed in Chinatown & LES

Chinatown and the Lower East Side have two active licensed dispensaries with about 1,130 products, plus The Flowery's Chinatown store on Canal Street, which was listed but publishing no menu at our last pull. CONBUD on Delancey Street near Essex is the larger at about 590 products, and Mighty Lucky on the Bowery near Houston lists about 540. The two are a ten-minute walk apart.

Pre-rolls lead at 344 listings, with 291 edibles, 265 vapes and 143 flower options. The 50-item concentrate selection is decent, and the 18 topicals and 21 tinctures are more than most neighborhoods this size. CONBUD carries one of the deepest Ruby Farms runs in the city at 40 products and a solid Eaton Botanicals selection, and both shops stock Eaton Botanicals more deeply than most. Prices start at $3.39 for 1906 Boost pouches and $10.17 for 5 Boro dime bags. For the whole stretch from Canal to Houston east of the Bowery, these are the two full-menu options.

Chinatown & LES FAQ
Is there a licensed dispensary in Chinatown?

The Flowery has a store at 70 Canal Street, but it showed no products at our last menu pull. The nearest active shops are Mighty Lucky on the Bowery and CONBUD on Delancey, both about a ten-minute walk from Canal Street.

Which is bigger, CONBUD or Mighty Lucky?

CONBUD at about 590 products, with a deeper Ruby Farms pre-roll selection. Mighty Lucky at about 540 is close behind. Both carry the main New York brands, so check both for a specific item.

Is CONBUD near the Delancey-Essex subway?

Yes. 85 Delancey is a block from the Delancey Street and Essex Street station on the F, J, M and Z, and a short walk from the Williamsburg Bridge.
