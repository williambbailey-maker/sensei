Weed in East & West Village

The East and West Village together have nine licensed dispensaries and about 3,400 products, the most shops of any Manhattan neighborhood we cover below 14th Street. Housing Works Cannabis Co. at 750 Broadway is the largest at about 850 products. Gotham on the Bowery lists about 550, The Flowery's East Village store on 10th Street about 510, The Flowery's West Village store on Christopher Street about 450 and PRYZM on Avenue B about 410. The smaller shops are Smacked on Bleecker Street (about 180), Dagmar on Jane Street (about 160), Bellanova at 331 East 9th Street (about 150) and Sky High Club on Avenue C (about 130).

Pre-rolls lead the combined menu at 980 listings, with 867 edibles, 687 flower options and 656 vapes, plus 128 concentrates. Housing Works has the longest brand list in the neighborhood and the most pre-rolls, flower, edibles and vapes, with MFNY and Florist Farms at the top of its list. Gotham Bowery is an MFNY and Ayrloom stop, PRYZM is built around pre-rolls and flower, and both Flowery stores lean on To The Moon. Dagmar is the pick for Ruby Farms, which leads its menu, and Smacked for Plug Play pods. Bellanova's list is led by Camino and MFNY. Because the stores are spread from the Hudson to Avenue C, the right choice depends mostly on which side of Broadway you are on.

Good to know
  • The Village has nine licensed shops: Housing Works on Broadway, Gotham Bowery, The Flowery East Village and PRYZM on the east side, The Flowery on Christopher Street, and smaller stores at Smacked, Dagmar, Bellanova and Sky High Club.
  • Pre-rolls (980) are the largest category, then edibles (867), flower (687) and vapes (656).
  • Housing Works at 750 Broadway is the deepest menu; east of Broadway it is Gotham Bowery and The Flowery East Village, and west it is The Flowery West Village.
  • PRYZM on Avenue B is second only to Housing Works for pre-rolls and flower.
East & West Village FAQ
Which Village dispensary is the largest?

Housing Works Cannabis Co. at 750 Broadway, with about 850 products. Gotham on the Bowery is second at about 550, followed by The Flowery East Village at about 510 and The Flowery West Village at about 450.

Is there a licensed dispensary in the West Village?

Yes. The Flowery on Christopher Street has about 450 products and is the largest shop west of Broadway. Smacked on Bleecker Street lists about 180, and Dagmar on Jane Street has about 160, heavy on pre-rolls, and is the westernmost shop in the neighborhood.

Where can I smoke after buying in the Village?

Not inside any dispensary, and not in Washington Square or Tompkins Square, which are parks. Smoking is allowed on sidewalks where tobacco is allowed. Edibles and drinks avoid the question.
