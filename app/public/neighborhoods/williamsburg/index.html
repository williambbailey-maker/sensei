Weed in Williamsburg

Williamsburg has six licensed dispensaries with about 1,800 products, the most shops of any Brooklyn neighborhood. Gotham's Domino store on Kent Avenue by the waterfront is the largest at about 515 products. The Flowery's Brooklyn store on Grand Street and Grams on Graham Avenue both list about 350, Sunflower on Metropolitan about 270, Dagmar on North 11th about 185 and Twisted Vibrations on Broadway in south Williamsburg about 135.

Pre-rolls lead at 504 listings, with 442 edibles, 407 vapes and 330 flower options, plus 80 concentrates. Grams carries 63 Florist Farms products, the deepest run of that brand anywhere we track, and the largest concentrate case in the neighborhood. The Flowery is the vape stop with Jaunty, STIIIZY and Fernway, and Dagmar is the Ruby Farms pre-roll shop. Prices start at $5 for 1906 Bliss drops and $5.31 for Layup and Ayrloom drinks, with 5 Boro dime bags at $9. The shops are spread from Kent Avenue to Graham Avenue to Broadway, so each serves its own corner: the waterfront, the L corridor and the south side.

Williamsburg FAQ
Which Williamsburg dispensary is closest to the Bedford L?

Dagmar at 61 North 11th Street is about five minutes west toward the river, and Gotham Domino on Kent Avenue is about ten minutes southwest. Sunflower on Metropolitan Avenue is a similar walk east toward Lorimer.

What is the biggest dispensary in Williamsburg?

Gotham Domino at 296 Kent Avenue, with about 515 products, near Domino Park. The Flowery Brooklyn and Grams are each about 350.

Is there a dispensary in south Williamsburg?

Twisted Vibrations at 301 Broadway, a block from the Marcy Avenue J, M and Z, with about 135 products and a flower-heavy menu. It is the only licensed shop we track south of Grand Street.
