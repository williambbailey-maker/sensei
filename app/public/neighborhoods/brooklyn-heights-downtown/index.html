Weed in Brooklyn Heights & Downtown

Brooklyn Heights, Downtown Brooklyn, Cobble Hill and Carroll Gardens have three active licensed dispensaries with about 1,150 products, plus Kaya Bliss on Henry Street, which was listed but publishing no menu at our last pull. The Travel Agency's Downtown Brooklyn store on Flatbush Avenue near Atlantic Terminal is the largest at about 560 products. Buzzy on Court Street lists about 380 and The Garden Club on Smith Street in Carroll Gardens about 210.

Edibles lead at 355 listings, with 270 pre-rolls, 243 vapes and 186 flower options, and the 56 concentrates, 24 tinctures and 18 topicals are all solid for a neighborhood this size. The Travel Agency carries the deepest Ruby Farms run in the city at 44 products and the deepest Camino run too. Buzzy has the largest concentrate case in the area, and The Garden Club is one of the few shops stocking Wizard Trees flower. Prices start at $10 for 1906 Boost+ pills and $12 for 2x Baked cookies, with 5 Boro blunts at $20. The three shops run north to south along Court, Smith and Flatbush, so there is one near every stop on the F and G between Bergen and Carroll.

Brooklyn Heights & Downtown FAQ
Is there a dispensary near Barclays Center?

The Travel Agency at 122 Flatbush Avenue is a few blocks from Barclays and Atlantic Terminal, with about 560 products. Be. on Flatbush in Park Slope is also close on the other side of the arena.

Which shop serves Carroll Gardens?

The Garden Club at 277 Smith Street, near Degraw, with about 210 products. It is the only licensed dispensary we track in Carroll Gardens; Buzzy on Court Street is a ten-minute walk north.

Why does Kaya Bliss show no products?

Our last menu pull returned no listings for Kaya Bliss on Henry Street. That usually means the shop had not published an online menu. Confirm directly with the store before visiting.
